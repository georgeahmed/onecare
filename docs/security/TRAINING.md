# Security & Privacy Training Program

Last updated: 2025-10-18  
Owners: Security, People Ops

## Objectives

- Ensure all engineers understand privacy obligations (PHI/PII), secure coding practices, and incident response procedures.

## Curriculum

- **Module 1:** Privacy fundamentals (data classification, consent, retention). 20 minutes.
- **Module 2:** Secure coding essentials (review `SECURE_CODING_NODE.md`, `SECURE_CODING_PY.md`, SSRF & logging guidelines). 30 minutes.
- **Module 3:** Secrets management & supply chain (Vault usage, rotation SLAs, SBOM/signing overview). 20 minutes.
- **Module 4:** Incident response basics (severity matrix, communication channels, how to escalate). 15 minutes.

Provide materials via short videos/slides; include comprehension quizzes.

## Schedule

- **Onboarding:** Within first two weeks for new hires/contractors.
- **Annual refresher:** Every 12 months (tracked on rolling basis).
- **Ad-hoc:** Following major incidents or policy updates.

## Tracking

- Maintain attendance/completion log in `docs/security/training-records/<year>.csv` with employee name, module completion dates, and reviewer.
- People Ops to remind managers of overdue training monthly.

## Responsibilities

- Security prepares content and updates modules as policies evolve.
- Engineering managers ensure team compliance.
- People Ops maintains records and schedules sessions.

## Feedback & Improvement

- Collect feedback after each session; integrate into next iteration.
- Track measurable outcomes (reduced security issues, improved checklist compliance).
