Task: IN-02.13 — Credential/cert rotation readiness (OAuth/mTLS refresh; hot-reload)

Context
- Keep GP Connect client available during credential or certificate rotations. Avoid downtime and minimize failed calls.

Files
- apps/booking/src/adapters/gpconnect.client.ts
- docs/USAGE.md (ops notes)

Steps
1) Implement token refresh logic before expiry (e.g., refresh at 80–90% of lifetime); cache and synchronize refresh to avoid stampedes.
2) Support mTLS certificate/key reload on SIGHUP or file watcher; atomically swap agents without dropping inflight requests.
3) Emit metrics: `auth.refresh.success/error`, `cert.reload.success/error` and log safe events (no secrets).
4) Document operational steps for rotating tokens/certs and how the client detects changes.

Acceptance Criteria
- Client continues operating across rotations; metrics/logs reflect events; no secrets in logs.

Validate
- Local simulation: update token/rotate cert files; observe smooth operation and metrics.
