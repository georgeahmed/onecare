Task: IN-01.13 — Health/readiness probes (FHIR/Object Store/OIDC)

Context
- Provide fast, cached health checks for outbound dependencies to integrate with service readiness endpoints without creating load storms.

Files
- apps/* services that use these clients (probe integration)
- packages/ports/src/fhir.ts, object-store.ts
- packages/security/src/index.ts (OIDC JWKS)

Steps
1) Add `checkHealth()` on FHIR client: attempt a lightweight metadata or HEAD with short timeout, cache result for 1–5s.
2) Add `checkHealth()` on Object Store: HEAD or signed-url verification with short timeout; cache likewise.
3) Add JWKS cache status and token-verify dry-run as OIDC health signal; cache result and include last-refresh time.
4) Document how to expose these signals via `/readyz` in consuming services.

Acceptance Criteria
- Health checks exist with caching; expose simple booleans and timestamps; no PHI in outputs.

Validate
- Integrate in one service’s `/readyz` and verify cached flips on dependency loss.
