Task: FE-01.22 — Responsive QA and device/browser matrix (mobile‑first)

Context
- Validate that the portal feels native on mobile, tablet, and desktop across a defined support matrix.

Files
- apps/portal/src/styles/*
- docs/USAGE.md (support matrix)

Steps
1) Define and document a device/browser support matrix (e.g., latest 2 versions of major browsers; iOS/Android versions).
2) Verify critical flows at mobile widths first; ensure inputs and hit areas are touch‑friendly.
3) Fix layout shifts and overflow; test in landscape; ensure safe-area insets respected.

Acceptance Criteria
- Matrix documented; manual checks pass on listed devices/browsers.

Validate
- Use responsive mode and at least one physical device for a quick smoke test.
