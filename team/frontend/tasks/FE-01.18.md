Task: FE-01.18 — Visual polish & micro-interactions (motion guidelines, skeletons)

Context
- Make interactions feel smooth and intentional. Use motion sparingly to communicate state without causing distraction or discomfort.

Files
- apps/portal/src/styles/tokens.css (motion tokens)
- apps/portal/src/components/ui/*

Steps
1) Add motion tokens (durations/easings). Keep durations short (150–200ms) for interactions; use reduced motion for prefers‑reduced‑motion.
2) Replace generic spinners with skeletons where possible; animate in subtly.
3) Add micro‑interactions (button press states, focus ring transitions) adhering to a11y.

Acceptance Criteria
- Motion consistent with tokens; reduced motion respected; skeletons in place for loading.

Validate
- Visual/manual review; ensure no motion jank or excessive animations.
