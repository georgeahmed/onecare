Task: FE-02.9 — Offline/spotty network UX (queue confirm, backoff, resume)

Context
- Ensure users can complete booking even with flaky connectivity by queueing confirm actions and resuming once online.

Files
- apps/portal/src/lib/offlineQueue.ts
- apps/portal/src/components/booking/ConfirmBooking.tsx
- apps/portal/src/styles/global.css
- apps/portal/src/i18n/messages/en.ts
- apps/portal/src/i18n/messages/es.ts
- apps/portal/test/offlineQueue.test.ts

Steps
1) If offline, queue the confirm payload with correlationId; show local confirmation intent and resume automatically when back online.
2) Use exponential backoff and show a gentle progress indicator; never lose the user’s selection.
3) Provide a manual retry and cancel path.

Acceptance Criteria
- Confirm actions reliably resume; UX remains calm and clear; no data loss.

Validate
- Simulate offline; confirm; reconnect and observe send; unit tests for queue behavior.
