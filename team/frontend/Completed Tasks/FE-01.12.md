Task: FE-01.12 — Progressive disclosure & wizard flow (branching; save/resume)

Context
- Reduce cognitive load by showing only what’s needed. Guide users through a short, clear multi‑step flow that adapts to answers.

Files
- apps/portal/src/application/wizard/* (new)

Steps
1) Define a small state machine for steps with branching rules; keep logic pure and testable (no network in step logic).
2) Save/resume: persist current step + draft; allow returning users to continue where they left off.
3) Provide step indicators with accessible labels; allow back navigation; preserve inputs.
4) Microcopy: short, friendly prompts; examples; avoid blame‑y tone.

Acceptance Criteria
- Branching works; progress persisted; a11y and microcopy standards met.

Validate
- Manual path tests; unit tests for branching logic.
