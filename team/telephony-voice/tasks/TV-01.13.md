Task: TV-01.13 — Health/readiness + graceful shutdown

Context
- Expose liveness/readiness endpoints reflecting ASR and Object Store health; drain in-flight streams on shutdown.

Files
- apps/telephony/src/index.ts (probe routes and shutdown hooks)
- apps/telephony/src/adapters/asr.client.ts, ivr.adapter.ts (health checks)

Steps
1) `/healthz`: always 200; `/readyz`: 200 only when ASR and Object Store are reachable (cached 1–5s). Keep probe responses minimal; no envelopes.
2) On SIGTERM/SIGINT: stop accepting new calls; allow in-flight to finish up to grace period; close adapters; emit `shutdown.*` metrics.
3) Provide gauges for `inflight.calls` and `asr.inflight`; log shutdown events safely.

Acceptance Criteria
- Probes accurate and cached; graceful shutdown within budget; metrics/logs present.

Validate
- Simulate ASR down; observe `/readyz` 503; restore → 200. Trigger shutdown under load; verify drain.
