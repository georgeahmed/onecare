Task: BE-05.5 — Idempotent referral and outcome processing

Context
- Prevent duplicate referrals and duplicate outcome processing with idempotency keys and TTL.

Files
- apps/pharmacy-router/src/application/pharmacy.state.ts
- packages/ports/src/idempotency.ts

Steps
1) Compute a referral idempotency key from (patientId, conditionCode, destination, date) and reserve before send; duplicates → 409 envelope.
2) For outcomes, dedupe by (referralId, status, code) with TTL; ensure exactly-once write-back and notifications.
3) Metrics: `pharmacy.idempotency.hit/miss`, `pharmacy.outcome.duplicate` with correlationId in logs.

Acceptance Criteria
- Exactly-once semantics for referral send and outcome apply; duplicates rejected or no-oped safely.

Validate
- Unit tests for concurrent referrals and duplicate outcomes.
