Task: BE-03.13 — DLQ and poison-message quarantine

Context
- Prevent stuck consumers and infinite retries; quarantine poison messages with minimal, actionable context.

Files
- packages/events/src/topics.ts (ensure DLQ topic)
- apps/triage/src/index.ts (retry policy)
- docs/EVENTS.md (reference)

Steps
1) Implement bounded retries (max 3, backoff+jitter). Classify retryable vs non-retryable errors.
2) On exceeding retries or non-retryable code, publish to DLQ with: error code, correlationId, envelope.id, topic, and truncated details (no PHI).
3) Emit metrics for `triage.dlq` and `triage.retry`. Consider a small backoff queue to avoid hot loops.
4) Document remediation guidance (how to inspect DLQ, replay safely after fix).

Acceptance Criteria
- Poison messages land in DLQ with safe context; retries bounded. Metrics/logs present.

Validate
- Unit tests simulating poison message path; assert DLQ publish and metrics.
