Task: BE-07.3 — Clinician API: Assign/Unassign

Context
- POST /clinician/tasks/:id/assign and /unassign with idempotency and audit.

Steps

3) Error envelopes; correlation headers; clinic scope enforced.

Acceptance Criteria

Validate
