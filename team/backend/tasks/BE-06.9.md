Task: BE-06.9 — Portal event DLQ & retries (bounded, idempotent)

Context
- Ensure reliable delivery of `portal.notify` events with bounded retries, jittered backoff, idempotency keys, and DLQ for poison messages.

Files
- apps/access-gate/src/application/portal.state.ts (publish path)
- packages/events/src/topics.ts (broker.dlq exists)
- docs/EVENTS.md (reference)

Steps
1) Apply retry policy: max 3 attempts with exponential backoff + jitter. Annotate envelope meta with `attempt`, `firstSeenAt`.
2) Use idempotency key computed in BE-06.4 on publish and honor on consume to avoid duplicates.
3) On non-retryable errors or retries exhausted, publish a minimal DLQ record to `broker.dlq` including `topic`, `error.code`, `correlationId`, and safe context.
4) Emit metrics: `event.retry`, `event.dlq`, `event.publish_error` tagged by topic and error code.
5) Unit tests: force failures to observe retries and DLQ; validate idempotency prevents duplicate handling.

Acceptance Criteria
- Retries bounded and jittered; DLQ receives poison messages with minimal context.
- Idempotency enforced on publish/consume paths; tests pass.
