Task: BE-06.10 — Health/readiness/liveness + graceful shutdown (schedulers)

Context
- Provide k8s-friendly health probes and graceful shutdown for access-gate and capacity loops. Drain in-flight operations safely.

Files
- apps/access-gate/src/index.ts (probe routes)
- apps/access-gate/src/scheduler.ts (shutdown hooks)
- apps/capacity/src/application/capacity.state.ts (drain coordination)

Steps
1) Add `/healthz` (liveness) and `/readyz` (readiness) endpoints. Readiness reflects scheduler initialized and error rates within bounds.
2) Implement SIGTERM/SIGINT handlers: stop scheduling new ticks, allow inflight to finish up to timeout (e.g., 10s), then exit.
3) Expose gauge `inflight.ticks`; emit metrics `shutdown.start`, `shutdown.timeout`.
4) Keep probe responses minimal; do not use full error envelope for them.
5) Document probes and shutdown in service README.

Acceptance Criteria
- Probes stable; readiness flips during startup/shutdown; drain within timeout.
- Metrics/logs recorded for shutdown events.

Validate
- Manual: run service, trigger SIGTERM, observe graceful exit in logs within timeout.
