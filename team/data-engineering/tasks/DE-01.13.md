Task: DE-01.13 — Backfill & reprocessing framework (range runs, job metadata, idempotency)

Context
- Build a safe framework to reprocess historical data or backfill gaps, with job metadata tracking and idempotent writes.

Current State
- No backfill CLI or ledger exists; rollup and quality scripts operate only on ad-hoc files without resumable metadata.

Files
- scripts/analytics_backfill.js (new)
- docs/ANALYTICS_BACKFILL.md (new)
- var/analytics/backfill-ledger.jsonl (generated)

Steps
1) Implement a CLI that executes over date ranges with adjustable parallelism and records job metadata (run id, params, partitions processed, status) in a durable ledger.
2) Ensure downstream writes are idempotent (reuse windowed upsert from DE-01.12 or storage planner from DE-01.9) and protect against duplicate processing.
3) Provide dry-run and resume support (checkpoint via ledger) and document operational safeguards + failure handling in the runbook.

Acceptance Criteria
- Backfill runs record metadata; idempotent; resumable; documented.

Validate
- Run a synthetic backfill on sample data; verify outputs and ledger entries.
