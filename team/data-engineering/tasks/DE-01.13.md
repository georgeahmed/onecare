Task: DE-01.13 — Backfill & reprocessing framework (range runs, job metadata, idempotency)

Context
- Build a safe framework to reprocess historical data or backfill gaps, with job metadata tracking and idempotent writes.

Files
- scripts/etl/backfill.ts or .py (new)
- docs/ANALYTICS_BACKFILL.md (new)

Steps
1) Implement a CLI to run over a date range with adjustable parallelism; record job metadata (run id, params, partitions processed, outcome) in a small ledger.
2) Ensure writes are idempotent (e.g., upsert by keys or overwrite partitions atomically); protect against duplicate processing.
3) Provide dry‑run and resume capabilities; document operational safeguards.

Acceptance Criteria
- Backfill runs record metadata; idempotent; resumable; documented.

Validate
- Run a synthetic backfill on small data; verify outputs and ledger.
