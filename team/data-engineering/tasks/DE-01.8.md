Task: DE-01.8 — Reliable consumer (at-least-once, idempotency keys, retries+jitter, DLQ)

Context
- Consume `analytics.metric` with at-least-once semantics. Prevent duplicates with idempotency, retry transient errors with jitter, and route poison messages to DLQ.

Files
- packages/bus/* (consumer wiring)
- packages/ports/src/idempotency.ts
- packages/events/src/topics.ts (Topics.analytics.metric, broker.dlq)

Steps
1) Create a durable subscription for `Topics.analytics.metric`. Parse `EventEnvelope<Metric>` and propagate `correlationId` to logs.
2) Derive or read an idempotency key (envelope.id or hash of name+labels+timestamp) and reserve before sink write; on conflict → ack and no‑op.
3) Apply max 3 retries with exponential backoff + jitter on transient errors; on non‑retryable or exhausted attempts, publish minimal DLQ record.
4) Emit metrics: `analytics.ingest.ok`, `analytics.ingest.error`, `analytics.ingest.retry`, `analytics.ingest.dlq`.

Acceptance Criteria
- Idempotent writes; retries bounded; DLQ path exists; metrics/logs present.

Validate
- Unit tests with fake bus and sink to simulate duplicates, transient and permanent errors.
