Task: DE-01.16 — Retention & lifecycle (TTL, cold storage tiering, vacuum)

Context
- Manage storage costs and privacy by enforcing retention TTLs, tiering older data, and cleaning up orphaned files.

Current State
- No retention ADR or automation exists for analytics artefacts; only the quarantine exporter manages archives.

Files
- docs/adr/2025-10-xx-analytics-retention.md (new)
- scripts/analytics_retention.js (new)

Steps
1) Define retention policy per dataset (raw events, rollups, lineage) with TTLs and regulatory notes; document in the ADR.
2) Implement a retention job that deletes or tiers partitions older than the TTL with dry-run safeguards and audit logging.
3) Add a vacuum/cleanup routine to remove orphaned files after compaction and document scheduling guidance.

Acceptance Criteria
- Policy documented; retention job implemented; safeguards in place.

Validate
- Run on synthetic partitions and verify deletions/tiering + logs.
