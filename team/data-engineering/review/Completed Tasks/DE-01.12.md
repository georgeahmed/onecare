Task: DE-01.12 — Incremental rollups (windowed p50/p95, idempotent upserts, backfill)

Context
- Compute incremental aggregates (counts, error rates, latency p50/p95) in sliding windows and upsert idempotently into rollup tables.

Current State

- `test/scripts/metrics_rollup.test.ts` covers window aggregation, idempotent writes, and directory-walking backfill execution.
