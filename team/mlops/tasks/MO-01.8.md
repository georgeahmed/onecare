Task: MO-01.8 — Rollout strategies: blue/green, canary, and shadow

Context
- Deploy ML services safely with traffic controls and fast rollback. Support blue/green switches, progressive canaries, and shadow evaluation.

Files
- charts/onecare/* or infra/k8s/* (new)
- docs/MLOPS_ROLLOUTS.md (new)

Steps
1) Blue/green: deploy vNext alongside vCurrent; route 0%→100% traffic switch via Ingress/Service selector; document cutover/rollback.
2) Canary: use progressive delivery (e.g., Argo Rollouts) to shift 1%→5%→25%→50%→100% with metric checks (latency/error); auto‑abort on breach.
3) Shadow: mirror % of traffic to vNext without impacting responses; record predictions/latency for analysis; no side effects.
4) Runbooks: promotion, rollback, and shadow analysis; include screenshots/commands.

Acceptance Criteria
- Blue/green/canary configs exist; shadow mirroring documented; rollback verified in staging.

Validate
- Deploy a dummy version; exercise canary and rollback; collect metrics.
