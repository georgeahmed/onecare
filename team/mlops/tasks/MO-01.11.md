Task: MO-01.11 — Model/version registry integration

Context
- Track model artifacts and metadata (hashes, metrics, thresholds) and control promotion from staging to prod.

Files
- docs/MODEL_REGISTRY.md (new)

Steps
1) Choose a registry (MLflow/Weights&Biases/artifact store) or define a lightweight manifest format; record model hash, size, training data version, metrics, and calibration data.
2) Wire deployment to pull artifacts by version/tag; verify checksums on startup.
3) Document promotion workflow with approval gates and rollback rules.

Acceptance Criteria
- Registry/manifest in place; deployments pull pinned versions; checksums verified; promotion runbook exists.

Validate
- Deploy vNext via registry; verify integrity and metadata.
