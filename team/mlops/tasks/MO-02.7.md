Task: MO-02.7 — Reliable ingestion (at-least-once, idempotency, retries+jitter, DLQ)

Context
- Ensure feature updates from events are ingested reliably and idempotently, with bounded retries and DLQ for poison updates.

Files
- scripts/feature_store/ingest_stream.ts or .py (extend)
- packages/ports/src/idempotency.ts

Steps
1) Subscribe to relevant topics; derive idempotency keys for feature mutations and reserve before write.
2) Retry transient errors with exponential backoff + jitter; cap attempts; on failure publish DLQ message with minimal context.
3) Emit metrics: `features.ingest.ok/error/retry/dlq` and `features.freshness.lag_ms`.

Acceptance Criteria
- Idempotent updates; retries bounded; DLQ present; metrics/logs PHI‑safe.

Validate
- Unit tests with fakes; playback harness for event streams.
